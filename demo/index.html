Drop-in Plugin Usage

Enable ASCII art anywhere on your web application simply with HTML attributes or one line of JavaScript:

<!-- 1. Include the UMD bundle from CDN or local --> <script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/hexpic/dist/index.umd.js"></script> <!-- 2. Add data-hexpic to any image tag --> <img src="avatar.png" data-hexpic data-hexpic-width="120" data-hexpic-dithering="atkinson" /> <!-- 3. Auto-convert with HexPic.init() --> <script> HexPic.init(); </script>
